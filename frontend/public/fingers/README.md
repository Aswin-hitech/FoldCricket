# Finger Notation Assets Directory

This folder is reserved for finger gesture images representing hand cricket numbers 1 to 10.

### Image Naming Convention:
- `finger_1.png` or `finger_1.jpg`
- `finger_2.png` or `finger_2.jpg`
- `finger_3.png` or `finger_3.jpg`
- `finger_4.png` or `finger_4.jpg`
- `finger_5.png` or `finger_5.jpg`
- `finger_6.png` or `finger_6.jpg`
- `finger_7.png` or `finger_7.jpg`
- `finger_8.png` or `finger_8.jpg`
- `finger_9.png` or `finger_9.jpg`
- `finger_10.png` or `finger_10.jpg`

The buttons in `ArenaPage.tsx` automatically look for `/fingers/finger_{n}.png` within `.fingerNotationSlot`. When you drop your graphics into this folder, they will immediately show above the number!
